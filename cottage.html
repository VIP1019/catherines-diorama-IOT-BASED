How it works

Reservation-linked activation
Reservation→ Arrival→ Smart Entrance→ NFC Check-in→ Cottage Light ON

The NFC reader at the entrance identifies the guest and verifies the reservation. The assigned cottage is saved to the system, and only that cottage's light turns on. Other cottages stay dark until their own guests check in.
